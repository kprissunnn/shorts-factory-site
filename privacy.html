Privacy Policy — Shorts Factory

Last updated: October 17, 2026

What the app does

Shorts Factory is used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, and to read the statistics of those videos.

Data accessed

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's accounts, used only to upload videos and read their statistics.
  • Basic account information (account name and ID) to confirm which account is being published to.
  • Public performance metrics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time).

Data storage

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository. Video statistics are stored in that same repository. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Third-party users

The app does not collect, store or process data about any other user, viewer or follower.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings (Google Account permissions, Instagram "Apps and websites", TikTok "Manage app permissions").
